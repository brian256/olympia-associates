Why we're building this — revised

Our hypothesis, now tested

Two problems, one shared root cause. We asked people who live on both sides of it — here's what held up, and what we got wrong the first time.

For people

It's not that opportunities don't exist. It's that nothing proves you're reliable.

  • "Canadian experience" came up unprompted from almost everyone we talked to
  • Being pre-screened as legitimate was the one thing that landed with everyone, every time
  • References and volunteer hours scattered across texts, memory, and old emails
For organizations

Screening eats the time you don't have — and still doesn't stop no-shows.

  • About half the organizations we talked to have no real screening process at all
  • The ones who screen hardest still see the most no-shows — more effort isn't the fix
  • Nobody's checking whether the organization posting is legitimate, either
Our idea

One shared, trusted place — built the way people actually said they'd use it.

  • A guided profile, not a chatbot interview — that idea tested flat, so we dropped it
  • Verification runs both ways: organizations get checked too, not just people
  • Starting here in Edmonton, with a direct line into the rest of Western Canada
What changed since last time: the biggest surprise wasn't a new idea — it was two old ones getting corrected. An AI-led chat intake sounded clever on paper; nobody asked for it. Verifying organizations sounded like a hard sell; every organization we asked said that seemed fair. We'd rather build what you actually want than what we assumed you would.
