How Career Review is built.

Done With You

We build the strategy together and run the campaign collaboratively.

We map your career preferences first — sector, org size, location, salary floor, role type. Then we build the assets together, target the employers, tailor each application, and adapt in real time. You're in it, not waiting on it. This model works across every client situation: a new market, a career change, internationally trained credentials, or a straightforward pivot. The accompaniment model means the thinking happens with you — not for you.

    Handoff or transition

What we do

Two ways to engage. Both scoped to the moment.

Strategy Session

A focused half-day on a specific problem or opportunity.

Preparation, a structured session, and a written output. Best for leaders who have a specific decision to make, a challenge to reframe, or a direction to pressure-test — and who need a structured conversation with someone who has done this before, not a sounding board.

The session is not a presentation. It is a working session — with frameworks applied to your actual situation, real-time analysis, and a written output that captures the thinking and the next steps.

Good for: a specific strategic decision, a challenge that needs reframing, or a plan that needs testing before commitment.

Project Engagement

A scoped engagement with defined deliverables and a clear end point.

For situations that require more than a half-day — a competitive analysis, a positioning framework, a full business plan, a digital presence build, a governance document. This is the format used for the Janzen Family Care and Haven Icecream engagements. It is what the accompaniment model was built for.

What working together looks like.

03

How the engagement is structured

Two zones. You start in one and move to the other.

Outcomes don't always arrive on a schedule, but a consulting engagement cannot run indefinitely, for your sake as much as ours. This is how every engagement, in either practice, is structured, and how we stay useful after the contracted work ends.

Zone 1 — Active Engagement

The contracted work. Defined scope. Full attention.

This is the paid engagement: a Career Review, a coaching series, a facilitation, a strategy session or a project. It runs for the scope and timeline in your contract. During this phase you have direct access to Brian Ssennoga and real-time adaptation as the work develops.

The active engagement ends when the scope is complete, not when the outcome arrives. For Career Review clients, most land during or shortly after this phase. Some do not, and that is handled honestly.

  • Deliverables built to the agreed scope
  • Application tailoring and employer targeting (Career Review)
  • Direct consultant access throughout
  • Coaching calls and interview preparation where in scope
Zone 2 — Olympia Community

Post-engagement support for Career Review. Light-touch. Ongoing.

When the active engagement concludes, Career Review clients who haven't yet landed transition into the Olympia Community — a WhatsApp-based professional group where job opportunities, resources, and market intelligence are shared on an ongoing basis.

This is not a paid service. It is a community of professionals who have gone through the same process. Job leads get shared. Referrals happen. The relationship continues — just in a different mode.

  • WhatsApp-based — low friction, high relevance
  • Curated job opportunities shared as they surface
  • Peer connections with professionals in similar transitions
  • Occasional group resources and workshops
  • No time limit — stay as long as it's useful

Why this structure exists: rather than extending the engagement indefinitely at full consulting rate — which creates a bad dynamic for both parties — the Community Zone keeps the relationship alive and useful without misrepresenting what a consulting engagement can promise. We cannot guarantee a job or a business outcome. We can guarantee the work, the tools, and continued connection after the contracted work is done.

Every engagement runs under one name: Brian Ssennoga, Olympia & Associates, Alberta trade name TN27173962. One person, one signature, one accountable name on everything you receive — check it against anything sent to you.
